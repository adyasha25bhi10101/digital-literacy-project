## Part B: Prevention Checklist
'Stay Safe Online' Checklist
1. UPI Receipt Rule: Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. If someone asks you
to "scan a QR code" or "enter your PIN" to get a payment, it is 100% a scam.
2. Verify 'Collect Requests': Before clicking "Pay" on apps like Phone Pe, Google Pay,
or Paytm, carefully read the notification. Scammers often send "Collect Requests"
disguised as cashback or refunds.
3. App-Based : Switch from SMS-based Two-Factor Authentication to an authenticator
app (like Google Authenticator) for your email and Instagram. This protects you from
"SIM Swapping" attacks.
4. Public Wi-Fi Hygiene: Avoid logging into your student portal or banking apps while
on campus or cafe public Wi-Fi. Use your mobile data or a trusted VPN for sensitive
tasks.
5. The 'Digital Arrest' Reality Check: Remember that no Indian law enforcement
agency (CBI, Police, or Customs) will ever "arrest" you via a Skype or WhatsApp
video call or demand money to "clear your name."
6. Masked Aadhaar: When submitting ID for college events or internships, use a
(which hides the first 8 digits) to prevent identity theft.
7. Social Media Privacy: Set your profiles to private and be skeptical of "urgent"
messages from friends asking for money—their accounts might be compromised.
Always call them to verify.

from oƯicial university websites or trusted stores. Pirated "cracked" software often

## Reporting Channels
If you or someone you know is a victim of cybercrime, report it immediately:
1. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
2. National Helpline Number: 1930 (Available 24/7)
