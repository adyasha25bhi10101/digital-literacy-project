Social Media Do’s : 
Use a professional profile picture
Think before posting any content on social media
Maintain a positive tone while using social media
Keep your information private
Use strong and secure passwords
Verify information before sharing it with others
Use polite language while interacting with other people on social media
Give credit to the original creator while sharing information or content with others
Keep your privacy settings updated
Share content that is meaningful and valuable
Be mindful of your audience while using social media
Use social media for learning purposes
Report inappropriate content to the authorities
Maintain a balance between your real life and online life
Follow the rules and regulations of the particular community or website

Social Media Don’ts:
Don’t share your personal information with people on social media
Don’t post offensive or abusive content on social media
Don’t spread false news or information on social media
Don’t fight or argue with people on social media
Don’t share every single thing about your life with people on social media
Don’t use weak or common passwords while creating an account on social media
Don’t post content or information without permission from the original creator
Don’t ignore your privacy settings while using social media
Don’t post content or information when you are feeling angry or emotional
Don’t follow unknown links or messages sent by unknown people
Don’t bully or troll people on social media
Don’t overuse or abuse social media
Don’t post illegal or inappropriate content on social media
Don’t believe everything that is shown or told on social media
Don’t harm your reputation by posting inappropriate content or information on social media
